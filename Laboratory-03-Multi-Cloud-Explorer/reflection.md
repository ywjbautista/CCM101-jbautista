# Mission Reflection

This activity gave me a better understanding of how AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud can serve different purposes even though they provide many similar cloud services. Among the three platforms, AWS impressed me the most. I became interested in it because of the variety of services it offers and how these services can support different types of applications and organizations. It is also the platform I would like to explore and experience using more in the future.

While comparing the three providers, I noticed that they all offer important services such as virtual machines, storage, networking, identity management, databases, and Kubernetes. However, I learned that having similar services does not mean that one provider is always the right choice. Business requirements have a major influence on the decision. The existing technologies, type of workload, expected growth, and specific goals of a client should be considered before recommending a platform.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant made this activity more interesting because I had to think from the client's point of view instead of simply choosing the platform I personally preferred. I learned to connect each situation with the strengths and services of the different providers. Using KillerCoda also helped me see actual Linux server information such as the operating system, CPU, memory, and disk space, making the activity more practical.

My GitHub portfolio has also improved through this laboratory because my research, comparisons, recommendations, screenshots, and practical work are organized in one place. Looking at the repository now makes it easier for me to see what I have completed and how my understanding of cloud computing has developed.
